UNDER THE HOOD

From student data to prediction.

The application follows a complete machine learning pipeline from ingestion and preprocessing to model selection and deployment.

01

Data Ingestion

Student data is loaded and divided into training and testing sets using an 80/20 split.

02

Preprocessing

Numerical features are scaled while categorical variables are imputed, encoded and transformed.

03

Model Selection

Multiple regression algorithms are evaluated and the strongest model is selected automatically.

04

Prediction

New student information passes through the saved preprocessing pipeline and trained model.

PREDICTION INPUTS

What influences the prediction?

◈

Academic Performance

Reading and writing scores provide direct academic signals for estimating mathematics performance.

◎

Student Profile

Gender and race/ethnicity are incorporated as categorical model features.

◇

Educational Context

Parental education, lunch type and test preparation provide additional context.
